# Mini Course Catalog

## Lesson 1: Chunking

Long documents are split into chunks before indexing. Chunking makes retrieval more
precise because a search result can point to the relevant passage instead of the
whole document.

## Lesson 2: Ranking

Ranking combines evidence from multiple scoring functions. A hybrid ranker can
weight dense and sparse scores with a parameter such as alpha.

## Lesson 3: Evaluation

Retrieval quality can be evaluated with queries, relevance labels, recall at k,
precision at k, and mean reciprocal rank.
